C64 DPainter for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer)

1. Drag "C64 DPainter.app" into your Applications folder.
2. The app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time.
   Open Terminal and run this once:

       xattr -cr "/Applications/C64 DPainter.app"

   Then start the app normally.

   Alternative without Terminal: try to open the app, then go to
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
