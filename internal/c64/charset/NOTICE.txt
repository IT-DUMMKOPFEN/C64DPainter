upper.bin, lower.bin: the character sets of the Commodore 64 character ROM
(upper case/graphics and lower case/upper case), 256 characters of 8 x 8
pixels each.

C64 character ROM (c) Commodore Business Machines. Used here, as in many
PETSCII editors (for example Petmate by Janne Hellsten, MIT license, from
whose repository the files were taken), to show and export PETSCII
pictures for the C64.
