C64 DPainter - Changelog
========================

Version 0.96 beta
-----------------

Layers
- A picture now has four layers: layer 1 is the template for tracing,
  layers 2 to 4 are paint layers.
- The layers are listed from 1 at the top to 4 at the bottom, in the order
  they lie on top of each other: layer 2 is in front, layer 3 behind it,
  layer 4 at the back.
- The multicolor rules apply to all paint layers together (hidden layers
  included), so every combination of visible layers is always a valid C64
  picture. The cell info marks colors that only come from hidden layers.
- New switch "Layers 3 + 4" next to the Layers title (also in the Layer
  menu). At the start only layer 2 is active and layers 3 and 4 are
  switched off. Once they are on, every layer can be shown or hidden on
  its own.
- Keys 2 / 3 / 4 choose the layer to paint on (3 and 4 switch the extra
  layers on). All tools, the selection, Auto dither, the Dither brush and
  Smear work on the active layer.
- Each page (spare page) has its own layers. Undo keeps the visibility and
  the active layer.

Eraser
- New Eraser tool (W): makes pixels of the active layer transparent again,
  with the normal brush shapes and Ctrl for straight lines.
- "Clear layer" empties the whole active layer.

Project files
- New file format .c64dp (C64 DPainter project): keeps all layers with
  their visibility, the active layer and the template with its settings.
- KLA, PNG and PRG still contain the visible layers merged into one
  picture; the program warns before layers would get lost.

Gradient fill (new tool, Shift+F)
- Fills the connected area under the mouse with a color gradient, on the
  active layer.
- A separate window shows all 45 gradients in three compact tabs:
  15 C64 color ramps as 3 colors (light -> dark), 5 colors
  (dark -> light -> dark) and 5 colors (light -> dark -> light).
- Shapes: Linear, Circle and Diamond.
- Single click: the gradient starts at the click point and reaches to the
  far edge of the area (linear in one of 8 preset directions).
- Click and drag: sets direction and width; a line shows it while you drag.
- Options: Reverse colors and Dithered transitions (clean 25 / 50 / 75 %
  patterns between the color bands, off by default).
- The side panel shows the chosen gradient and a button to open the
  window again.

Other changes
- Key 0 fits the picture to the window (like Ctrl+0 and Home).
- About window: supported systems, a thank-you to all users and a full
  list of sources with thanks.
- macOS: the app is now built for Apple silicon (M chips) only.
- Windows 11 is the supported Windows version (Windows 10 may crash).
- Help pages updated.


Version 0.95 beta
-----------------
- Dither styles reduced to Artist, Scanlines, Checker and Stripes.
- New dither style "Color": shades by dithering with a darker color that is
  already in the 4x8 cell (or fits into a free slot). Auto dither darkens
  towards darker neighbors, the Dither brush darkens what you paint over
  (Darkness 25 / 50 / 75 %).
- Auto dither and the Dither brush are marked as experimental.
- New Smear brush (Shift+S): drags the colors along the stroke like a
  smudge tool, with a strength setting; full cells use the nearest cell
  color, so the multicolor rules always hold.
- New dithering engine: stepped transitions like pixel artists do it -
  two colors per transition, a few clean stages (50 %, 25 %, 12 %),
  outlines, thin lines and small details stay sharp, only color pairs that
  artists really mix are blended. New settings: Width, Steps, Keep edges,
  Details, Artist pairs.
- Help and README updated.


Version 0.9 beta
----------------
- New Edit > Auto dither (Shift+A): blends the borders between flat color
  areas, with a live preview (dithered / original / split / changes), for
  the whole picture or the selection.
- New Dither brush (Shift+D): the same, only where you paint, with a
  preview of the pixels it would change.
- Dither styles reconstructed from 16 hand-pixelled C64 pictures.
- Color pair affinities measured from the pictures ("color ramps").
- Cell color modes Keep / Add / Re-choose; Add never causes color
  conflicts.
- Help pages, README and version updated.
