C64 DPainter - Changelog
========================

Version 0.96 beta
-----------------

Layers
- A picture now has four layers: layer 1 is the template for tracing,
  layers 2 to 4 are paint layers.
- The layers are listed from 1 at the top to 4 at the bottom, in the order
  they lie on top of each other: layer 2 is in front, layer 3 behind it,
  layer 4 at the back.
- The multicolor rules apply to all paint layers together (hidden layers
  included), so every combination of visible layers is always a valid C64
  picture. The cell info marks colors that only come from hidden layers.
- New switch "Layers 3 + 4" next to the Layers title (also in the Layer
  menu). At the start only layer 2 is active and layers 3 and 4 are
  switched off. Once they are on, every layer can be shown or hidden on
  its own.
- Keys 2 / 3 / 4 choose the layer to paint on (3 and 4 switch the extra
  layers on). All tools, the selection, Auto dither, the Dither brush and
  Smear work on the active layer.
- Each page (spare page) has its own layers. Undo keeps the visibility and
  the active layer.

Eraser
- New Eraser tool (W): makes pixels of the active layer transparent again,
  with the normal brush shapes and Ctrl for straight lines.
- "Clear layer" empties the whole active layer.

Project files
- New file format .c64dp (C64 DPainter project): keeps all layers with
  their visibility, the active layer and the template with its settings.
- KLA, PNG and PRG still contain the visible layers merged into one
  picture; the program warns before layers would get lost.

Gradient fill (new tool, Shift+F)
- Fills the connected area under the mouse with a color gradient, on the
  active layer.
- A separate window shows all 45 gradients in three compact tabs:
  15 C64 color ramps as 3 colors (light -> dark), 5 colors
  (dark -> light -> dark) and 5 colors (light -> dark -> light).
- Shapes: Linear, Circle and Diamond.
- Single click: the gradient starts at the click point and reaches to the
  far edge of the area (linear in one of 8 preset directions).
- Click and drag: sets direction and width; a line shows it while you drag.
- Options: Reverse colors and Dithered transitions (clean 25 / 50 / 75 %
  patterns between the color bands, off by default).
- The side panel shows the chosen gradient and a button to open the
  window again.

Shade brush (new tool, Shift+W)
- Lightens (left mouse button) or darkens (right mouse button) the colors
  under the brush by one step.
- Each pixel changes only once per stroke, so you can paint over an area
  without it turning white or black.
- Steps: all 16 colors by brightness, or one of the 15 color ramps of the
  gradient fill. Colors outside the chosen ramp stay as they are.

Symmetry (Shift+X)
- Everything you paint is also painted mirrored: left <-> right,
  top <-> bottom or both (four-fold).
- The axes are shown as dashed lines. They lie in the middle of the
  picture by default and can be moved in the side panel.
- Works with dots, freehand, line, curve, airbrush, dither brush, shade
  brush, eraser, all shapes, fill and gradient fill.
- Toolbox button and View > Symmetry.

New shapes, outlined and filled
- Square (N / Shift+N): drag from corner to corner, square on screen.
- Triangle (I / Shift+I), Hexagon (O / Shift+O) and Star (L / Shift+L):
  drag from the center; the direction sets the rotation, Shift keeps the
  shape upright. All shapes are regular on screen, like the circle.
- New icons; the rectangle icon is now clearly wider than the square icon.

Mirrored view (Shift+M)
- Shows the picture mirrored left <-> right for a fresh look at it.
  Only the view changes, the picture stays as it is, and you can keep
  painting.

CRT preview window (Shift+V)
- A floating window that shows the picture with its border like on a TV.
  It does not block the program: keep painting, it follows every change.
- Sizes 100 % (384 x 272, the real size) and 200 %.
- CRT on/off: scanlines, soft picture and the PAL color bleed, or sharp
  pixels.

Selection
- Dragging a selection now moves a copy: the original stays in the
  picture. Hold Shift when you start dragging to move it (the old place is
  cut out).

Working folder and quick save
- At the start and with File > New the program asks for the working
  folder where pictures and data are saved. The save and open dialogs
  start there; File > Working folder ... changes it at any time.
- F2 saves the picture at once as a numbered PNG (picture_0001.png,
  picture_0002.png ...) into the working folder. Numbering continues after
  the highest number in the folder; a picture that already has a file name
  uses that name.

About
- Multipaint (Marq/Kameli) added to the inspirations.

More gradients
- 12 new three-color ramps: Neon yellow (yellow - cyan - blue), Mint,
  Toxic, Sunset, Peach, Skin, Rust, Ember, Night sky, Lavender,
  Sand/Stone and Slate. They also appear in the two five-band tabs
  (dark - light - dark and light - dark - light).
- New tab "4 colors" (14 ramps) and "5 colors" (13 ramps) with four or
  five different colors from light to dark, sorted by the brightness of
  the C64 colors.
- The gradient window now has five tabs and a note on the C64 rule: a
  cell can only hold 3 colors plus the background, so 4- and 5-color
  gradients work best with wide color bands.

Shade brush
- The 4- and 5-color ramps can be chosen as steps too (finer shading).

CRT preview
- Sizes 100 % and 200 % (300 % removed).

Brushes menu (new)
- Stamps ..., Load brush ..., Save brush ..., Selection as brush,
  Standard brush, flip / rotate / resize the brush and "Brush keeps its
  own colors".
- Brushes are saved as PNG with a transparent background (every
  multicolor pixel two pixels wide, so other paint programs show them
  right). Loading accepts double-wide and single pixels; other colors
  become the nearest C64 color. The dialogs start in the folder "brushes"
  in the working folder.

Stamps (new, toolbox button)
- A window with 156 silhouettes: 35 people, 41 animals and 80 objects.
- Sizes 32 x 32, 32 x 64, 64 x 32 and 64 x 64 screen pixels (16 or 32
  multicolor pixels wide, 32 or 64 high), drawn with the wide multicolor
  pixels in one color with a transparent background - they always fit the
  multicolor rules.
- Tabs People, Animals, Objects and My brushes (the brushes saved in the
  brushes folder), a size filter, and the name and size of the chosen
  stamp.
- The chosen stamp becomes the brush and the tool switches to Dots: every
  click stamps once, in color 1 (left button) or color 2 (right button).
  X, Y, Z and H flip, rotate and resize it.

Other changes
- Key 0 fits the picture to the window (like Ctrl+0 and Home).
- About window: supported systems, a thank-you to all users and a full
  list of sources with thanks.
- macOS: the app is now built for Apple silicon (M chips) only.
- Windows 11 is the supported Windows version (Windows 10 may crash).
- Help pages updated.


Version 0.95 beta
-----------------
- Dither styles reduced to Artist, Scanlines, Checker and Stripes.
- New dither style "Color": shades by dithering with a darker color that is
  already in the 4x8 cell (or fits into a free slot). Auto dither darkens
  towards darker neighbors, the Dither brush darkens what you paint over
  (Darkness 25 / 50 / 75 %).
- Auto dither and the Dither brush are marked as experimental.
- New Smear brush (Shift+S): drags the colors along the stroke like a
  smudge tool, with a strength setting; full cells use the nearest cell
  color, so the multicolor rules always hold.
- New dithering engine: stepped transitions like pixel artists do it -
  two colors per transition, a few clean stages (50 %, 25 %, 12 %),
  outlines, thin lines and small details stay sharp, only color pairs that
  artists really mix are blended. New settings: Width, Steps, Keep edges,
  Details, Artist pairs.
- Help and README updated.


Version 0.9 beta
----------------
- New Edit > Auto dither (Shift+A): blends the borders between flat color
  areas, with a live preview (dithered / original / split / changes), for
  the whole picture or the selection.
- New Dither brush (Shift+D): the same, only where you paint, with a
  preview of the pixels it would change.
- Dither styles reconstructed from 16 hand-pixelled C64 pictures.
- Color pair affinities measured from the pictures ("color ramps").
- Cell color modes Keep / Add / Re-choose; Add never causes color
  conflicts.
- Help pages, README and version updated.
